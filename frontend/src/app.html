BEFORE YOUR FIRST REVIEW

Keep the process clear.

Does this detect defects automatically?

No validated defect model is enabled in this release. The application measures image quality. You inspect the photograph, record observations, and make the review decision.

Are my photographs stored?

Photographs are sent to the quality API for processing in memory. This application does not save them on the server. Photos and draft reviews remain in the current tab until you leave or close it.

What is in the export?

The JSON export contains completed reviews with image measurements, your observations, costs, estimated recovery, eligible alternatives, approval time. It does not include the original image files. Export before closing your session.

What can I use the sample for?

The generated bracket image lets you try uploading, quality checks, manual observations, and export. It is an illustration for exploring the workflow, not validated inspection or training data.
